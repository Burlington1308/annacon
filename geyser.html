Frequently Asked Questions

What are the signs that my geyser needs repair?

Common indicators include inconsistent water temperatures (failing thermostat or element), rusty or discoloured water (indicating internal tank corrosion), low water pressure, or unusual noises like hissing and banging.

Do you offer emergency repair services?

Yes, we provide emergency repair services for urgent issues. Please contact us directly for immediate assistance.

Should I repair or replace my geyser?

Minor issues like a burnt-out heating element, leaking valves, or a faulty thermostat can be repaired. However, if the main storage tank is cracked, leaking from the outer casing, or over 8 to 12 years old, full replacement is usually the safest and most cost-effective solution.

What is a sacrificial anode and why does it matter?

A sacrificial anode is a metal rod inserted into the geyser designed to corrode before the inner tank does. To prevent rust and prolong your geyser's lifespan, this rod should generally be inspected and replaced every 18 to 24 months.

Can I fix a leaking geyser myself?

DIY geyser repairs are strongly discouraged. Dealing with a burst geyser involves high-pressure water and electricity. Improper handling can exacerbate the issue, invalidate warranties, or pose severe safety risks.
