Frequently Asked Questions

Why is it leaking water?

Heat pumps naturally produce condensation as they operate. However, if the water level in the pool is dropping, or if the water around the unit smells like pool chemicals, you may have a cracked internal pipe or seal.

Do you offer emergency repair services?

Yes, we provide emergency repair services for urgent issues. Please contact us directly for immediate assistance.

Why is the unit making a screeching, buzzing, or grinding noise?

Fan issues: Screeching noises often point to a bad fan motor bearing.


Electrical problems: Buzzing noises may indicate electrical issues, such as a failing capacitor or relay.


Compressor problems: Buzzing or grinding noises may indicate compressor issues, which require professional attention.

Why is the unit running but not heating?

Check the filter: A dirty pool filter restricts water flow, meaning the heat pump won't activate. Clean or backwash your filter.


Check the thermostat: Make sure the set temperature is higher than the current water temperature.


Check for airflow blockages: Ensure the evaporator coils aren't blocked by leaves or dirt, as the unit needs good airflow to extract heat.


Low refrigerant: If the unit is blowing air that feels to colder than the outside temperature, it is likely working, but low refrigerant levels or a blocked system could be preventing the water from heating. Call a professional to check for leaks.
